Exact theorem
=============

Every twelve-system subfamily of the authenticated Etzion--Hartman 15-core is
excluded. Therefore any LS(3,4,20), if one exists, shares at most eleven of
these fifteen systems.

Coverage partition:

  30  exact ten-point DSATUR exclusions
   2  legacy point-0 DRAT exclusions
 423  batch point-0 DRAT exclusions
 ---
 455  all C(15,3) drop-three cases

This is not a proof that LS(3,4,20) is nonexistent and is not a resolution of
Erdos--Rosenfeld Problem #835.

Packing statement
=================

All 455 point-0 leaves contain the canonical three discarded STS(19)s.

In a five-fold leave, four disjoint STS(19)s use four of the five available
triples on every pair. The unused triples therefore form a forced fifth
STS(19). UNSAT of the five-colouring CNF is consequently an upper bound of
three on the point-link STS packing number.

Thus the packing number is exactly three in the 425 DRAT-certified cases.
The separate ten-point theorem does not supply that upper bound for its 30
cases.
