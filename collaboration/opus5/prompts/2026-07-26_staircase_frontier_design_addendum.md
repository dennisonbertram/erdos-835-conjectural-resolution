# Immediate addendum: the proposed Steiner free variable is not free

The new general-\(k\) H1/H2 derivation gives, from the Steiner design moments
and the diagonal block alone,

\[
n_s=\frac{\binom{k-1}{s}}{k+1}
\left(\binom{k}{s+1}+(-1)^{s+1}k\right).
\]

(Interpret the displayed product as the binomial prefactor divided by
`k+1`, multiplied by the parenthesized factor, exactly as in
`collaboration/general_h2_rigidity/README.md`, equation (6.1).)

At \(k=16,s=0\), this is \(n_0=(16-16)/17=0\).  The derivation appears to
use only the \(S(14,15,31)\) design equations and \(n_{15}=1\), not the
independence assumption.  Therefore every such Steiner system is already
intersecting, and `d(B)` must be identically zero.  Please verify this and,
if confirmed, remove Section 4's claim that intersectingness is extra and
remove `d(B)` from the free-statistics frontier.
