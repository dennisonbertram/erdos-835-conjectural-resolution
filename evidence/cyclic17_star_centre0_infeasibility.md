# The centre-0 star is infeasible: the fixed-Wallis cyclic-17 ansatz is excluded

Date: 2026-07-26.  Artifacts: `evidence/cyclic17_star_centre0/` (sources,
certificates, canonical family store).  Status: **certified negative
result for the fixed-Wallis, \(\mathbb Z_{17}\)-equivariant ansatz of
`cyclic17_equivariant_reduction.md`.  NOT a result about other golf
designs \(G(17)\), other cyclic starters, non-equivariant colourings, or
Erdős–Rosenfeld #835.**

## Theorem

The centre-0 star of the fixed-Wallis cyclic ansatz — fourteen
simultaneously exact fixed-pair slices \(\{0,j\}\), \(j=1..14\), with
per-orbit distinct phases at the centre — has **no solution**.
Consequently the full joint moving-triple layer, and with it the entire
fixed-Wallis cyclic-17 ansatz, is infeasible (every joint layer
restricts to a solution of all fifteen stars).

Sharper structure: with respect to the complete solution families
\(\mathcal R_{0,j}\),

- **every subset of at most five outer rows is compatible** —
  witnessed: one explicit compatible selection for each of the
  \(\binom{13}5=1{,}287\) five-subsets of \(\{2..14\}\) (and all
  smaller subsets, which restrict from these);
- **1{,}712 of the 1{,}716 six-subsets of \(\{2..14\}\) are
  incompatible** (the four exceptions: \(\{2,3,4,6,8,11\}\),
  \(\{2,3,5,8,10,11\}\), \(\{2,5,6,7,10,13\}\), \(\{2,4,5,8,11,14\}\));
  the canonical minimal core is \(\{2,3,4,5,6,7\}\).

So the minimal incompatible core size is exactly six, the obstruction
is genuinely six-fold (no smaller certificate exists), and it already
lives inside \(\{0,2\}..\{0,14\}\) — family \(\{0,1\}\) is unnecessary.

## Scope, restated

Excluded: the fixed-Wallis golf design with \(\mathbb Z_{17}\)
translation equivariance (the "ansatz" of
`cyclic17_equivariant_reduction.md` §4–5, scope 2).  Not excluded:
other golf designs \(G(17)\) (the Wallis choice was an ansatz, not
WLOG), non-cyclic or partially equivariant colourings, and the
unrestricted problem.  **#835 is not solved.**  The natural next
question is whether the six-fold local obstruction mechanism persists
across other \(G(17)\) starters — if it does uniformly, the whole
cyclic-17 route closes; if not, the surviving starters are the search
frontier.
